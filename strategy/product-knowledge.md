# Product knowledge for marketing (from the live system, 25 Nov 2026)

Source: officeflow.measureflow.com.au /roadmap/ (repo ai-office-flow, web/public/roadmap/index.html), the demo,
the ecosystem site and Paul's accepted proposal. Use the trade's own words below in copy - it proves Paul knows
how a kitchen business really runs.

## The Job Roadmap / Business Tree (/roadmap/)
- A 10-minute tool: the owner starts from AOF's standard kitchen job flow (and bathrooms), then ticks, moves,
  renames, adds or deletes steps. A "Business Tree" grows as they tick. They send it in and it becomes the
  roadmap for their build and the basis of their quote ("your quote comes from your own roadmap").
- Captures: business name, contact, people in the business, what runs their jobs now, other job types
  (laundries, wardrobes, commercial), software it must work with, what's hard today.
- Sending = "Email it to us" (downloads a file + opens their email - they must attach and send) or download/print.
  Marketing gap: no direct submit, no r/utm tracking, a lead can drop off at the attach step.

## The kitchen job, step by step (standard flow)
Front office
1. Enquiry in by website chat (AI), phone or walk-in -> job number K1235-SURNAME (kitchen + bathroom = K1235 and B1235).
2. Measure and design visit: first free designer from the diary; customer and designer texted.
3. Showroom appointment; customer reminded the day before; first draft design, colours finalised, quote presented.
4. Benchtop quote: plan emailed to the stone fabricator, who uploads their price by link onto the job.
5. Contract: quote becomes contract (Q2 -> Q2C); colours, handles, finishes, appliances; anything not decided is
   TBC (yellow) and chased every morning until confirmed.
6. Signed online, on the showroom kiosk, or on paper: job Won; payment stages into Xero with the job number;
   Stage 1 deposit on signing.
7. Cabinet Vision file uploaded and sent to the check measurer.
8. Check measure booked and done; check-measured file (-CM) back; programmers emailed straight away.
9. Final drawings signed online; Stage 2 sent on signing. Variations signed before they go ahead.
Back office / factory
10. Programmed for the CNC: jobs queued by delivery date, scheduler can push a job to the top (Urgent).
11. Materials list: the Cabinet Vision material summary loaded and matched to items, suppliers and prices.
12. Materials ordered: stock off the shelf, stock orders, special orders by supplier with the job number;
    routes: supplier-made (e.g. doors), board cut here, our spray booth, or sent to the painter.
13. Install and trades booked in Google Calendar; trade work schedule.
14. Factory sheet and job packs; deliveries ticked in; factory and site packs fill as files arrive.
15. Production steps ticked in the factory diary; urgent (K1235-U) and redo (K1235-R) items red at the top.
16. Before installation: Stage 3 sent 5-7 days before install.
17. Delivery and installation; any date change is a personal call to the customer.
18. Final site inspection; final invoice; Complete when paid in Xero ticks it off.
Owner can move any stuck step by hand, with a reason, kept on the job.
Bathrooms: own job type and form, no check measure, joinery through the factory if any, components ordering,
payment stages on rough-in, tiling and finish.

## Marketing angles from this
- Made to measure: the roadmap IS the measure. "We measure your business the way you measure a kitchen."
- Specific pains it kills, in their words: TBC items chased every morning; the check-measured file straight to
  the programmer; stone quotes uploaded by the fabricator; urgent/redo parts at the top; "where are we up to?"
  answered by the customer's own app.
- For visits: Paul can open the roadmap on an iPad and map the owner's process with them in 10 minutes - it gets
  the owner talking about their own business and produces the quote basis on the spot.

## The rest of the ecosystem (from ecosystem.measureflow.com.au, 25 Nov 2026)
One job number carries through: enquiry (AI Office Flow) -> site survey (Measure) -> two concept renders
(Render AI) -> contract, Cabinet Vision files, factory and install (AI Office Flow) -> 90-day care visit
(CabinetCare) -> content published (MeasureFlow Studio) -> new enquiries back into AI Office Flow.
"One job becomes twelve business assets." Site headline: "Measured once. Marketed everywhere."

- Measure (measureflow.com.au/measure): site-survey software for kitchen, wardrobe and laundry studios, site
  checkers and estimators - room sketch (walls, doors, windows, services), client/spec checklist by job type,
  one-tap DXF/PDF/CSV export incl. to-scale DXF for SketchUp, branded PDF info sheet with photos.
  Tagline: "Measure on site. Flow to result."
- Render AI (measureflow.com.au/ai): two concept renders built from the survey (room shape and size from the
  sketch, client's finishes and layout), 4K photoreal finals once the client picks; includes Measure.
  Must always carry: renders are concept visuals, not measured drawings - confirm dimensions from the survey.
- CabinetCare (cabinetcare.com.au, partner portal): returns ~90 days after install to adjust the joinery,
  documents the visit (90-Day Completion Report), professional photos with the homeowner's consent flow into
  Studio, neutral Google review request. Kitchen company adds it to their quote, logs the install, CabinetCare
  books the client ~day 70. Kitchen Care Program for ongoing care. Serves Coffs Harbour to Taree.
- MeasureFlow Studio: AI marketing for kitchen and joinery studios - finished jobs in, AI writes captions, SEO,
  blog and Houzz copy and before/after reels, one approve queue, publishes to Instagram, Facebook, Houzz, blog,
  Google review prompts; Studio+ AI Agent coming soon.
- Prices seen in another brief (UNCONFIRMED - check measureflow.com.au/#pricing before using): Measure tool
  about $20-199/month, Studio about A$169-599/month, CabinetCare $350 inc GST per kitchen.
