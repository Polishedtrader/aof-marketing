# Business review - AI Office Flow marketing (25 Nov 2026)

First whole-business look, for Paul and the Strategist. Reviewed: ecosystem.measureflow.com.au (repo
measureflow-ecosystem-site), officeflow /preview/apps/ and /ecosystem/ (repo ai-office-flow), the email campaign,
competitors (web). NOT reviewed (couldn't be opened from here): measureflow.com.au, cabinetcare.com.au.

## Biggest gaps
1. The email's landing page is a dead end. /preview/apps/ (where the campaign button goes) has no link to the
   full demo, the roadmap or the booking form - only "reply to the email, or call Paul". Add "See the whole
   system" (-> /ecosystem/interface/) and "Book a 15-min look with Paul" (-> ecosystem booking form, carrying r).
   Owner: AI Office Flow code chat.
2. Warm leads aren't being used. CabinetCare partner kitchen companies and MeasureFlow Measure/Render/Studio
   customers already know Paul. They should be worked before cold emails (personal note or visit), not mixed in.
3. No proof anywhere. Two sales, but no testimonial, case study or "running at X business" line on any site or
   email. One named customer quote (with written OK) is worth more than any copy change.
4. No price signal. Competitors publish a starting price (e.g. CabiPro from about A$200 on Capterra). AOF says
   "quote from your roadmap". A "builds typically from $X + support from $Y/month" line would filter and reassure.
5. Message mismatch. The ecosystem home page leads with the whole ecosystem ("Measured once. Marketed
   everywhere.") while the emails sell the customer/installer apps and the AI front desk. A cold owner who
   clicks through sees five products. A focused AI Office Flow landing page (or the hero changing when ?r= is
   present) would match the email.

## Positioning vs competitors
Australian kitchen/joinery software is mostly production/ERP on subscription: Jobman (end-to-end ERP for cabinet
makers, integrates with Cabinet Vision), CabiPro (job management for cabinet makers/joinery), WorkGuru, plus
general trade apps (Tradify, OctopusPro). AOF should NOT fight on factory/ERP. It wins on:
- front of house: AI website chat 24/7, reception, every enquiry numbered;
- customer and installer apps with the business's own logo ("where are we up to?" calls stop);
- built for their process, in their own name and accounts, set up and run for them;
- built by someone who ran a kitchen business for 25+ years.
Owners already on Jobman/CabiPro are a fit for the front-of-house part - don't treat them as lost.
