# 90-day marketing plan

Kept by the Strategist. Version 1, 25 Nov 2026. Covers 25 Nov 2026 - 28 Feb 2027.

## Goal
3 new signed builds by 28 Feb 2027 (5 in total), from 15+ face-to-face owner conversations.
Why these numbers: Paul builds each system himself, so 1 a month is about right for capacity; a first-meeting-to-sale
rate around 1 in 5 is a reasonable guess for a high-trust, owner-sold product. We'll replace the guess with real
numbers from `reports/visits.md` as soon as there are 10 visits.

## Reality check
- Dec is the pre-Christmas rush; most shops shut late Dec to mid Jan. Realistically: get proof and partners lined up
  in Dec, and push hard on visits from 12 Jan. Expect most of the 3 sales in Feb.
- Email is a warm-up, not the sales engine: ~20 first emails a month → 0-1 replies. The sale happens when Paul
  is in front of the owner.

## Channels, in priority order
1. **Warm introductions**: referrals from the two existing customers (Paul asks them himself) and supplier reps.
   Highest sales per hour.
2. **Paul's visits after the two emails**: owners only, per `reports/visits.md`. The core sales motion.
3. **Proof**: one named quote or short video from a customer, used in emails, visits and social.
4. **Cold email**: 1 a day, NSW Mid North Coast first. Keep it running and judge it after 20 sends (about 11 Dec).
5. **Social, 3 posts a week**: supports credibility; don't expect direct sales from it.
6. Later (Feb+): trade bookkeepers/accountants, ACFA, trade media story, a local demo night.
