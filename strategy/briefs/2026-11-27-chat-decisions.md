## 1. Price line: approved

Use it word for word in the chat, on the website, in emails and in scripts:

> **"Builds start at $6,990. Support plans start at $199 a month."**

- If asked "how much exactly?": the build price depends on the size of the business and what it needs. Offer the
  15-minute look with Paul to get a fixed price.
- Never quote another customer's price or name a customer.

## 2. Cabinet Vision and design software: approved

> **"AI Office Flow works with whatever design software you already use, for example Cabinet Vision."**

- Design software is not the competition. Never suggest replacing it or say anything against it.
- If asked about another program (Mozaik, Polyboard, 20-20 and so on): yes, it fits in alongside it. Offer the
  15-minute look to show how.

## 3. Also approved, OK to use

- "You build kitchens made to measure. Your office system should be too."
- "We train your staff on the system and on using AI in their jobs."
- "The system is already built. Your price is for fitting it to your business."
