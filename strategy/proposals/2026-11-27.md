**If the owner isn't in:**

(Leave no brochure, per your owners-only rule. Get the owner's best time and call back.)

**How we measure it:** shops visited, owners met, sit-downs booked, logged in `reports/visits.md`.

## Proposal 2: Two supplier reps as door-openers (Mid North Coast)

one board/hardware supplier and one benchtop/stone or appliance supplier. Ask them to mention you when an owner
complains about admin, quoting or "where are we up to?" calls, and to pass names. Offer a thank-you per sale.

**Why now:** reps see our buyers every week, in person, and the owners trust them. If the owner's shop is busier and
better run, the rep sells more. Suppliers already put money into reaching these owners (Planit sponsors ACFA events,
and Biesse runs open houses for cabinet makers), so a rep passing on a useful contact is normal in this trade.
You know who the good local reps are better than any list does. Two coffees before the Christmas break means
intros can start when shops reopen in January.

**First steps**
1. You pick 2 reps you already know or deal with (tell this chat who, so the team can track it).

**What to say (about 2 minutes)**

> the shop's own logo, quotes signed online, payments into Xero. It stops the 'where are we up to?' calls.

> their name? If they go ahead, I'll look after you: [$500 / a thank-you you set] per sale. No selling for you to do,

**What you have to do:** 2 coffees, about 2 hours including travel, before 18 Dec. Then 5 min a week to follow up any names.

## Proposal 3 (Paul asked 25 Nov): targeted paid ads to owners, 4-week test from 12 Jan

**The idea:** spend only on the owners we already know about (326 named businesses), not on broad audiences.
- **LinkedIn, ~$25/day:** upload our company list and target Owner/Director/Founder titles at those companies.
  Ad: a 30-45 s video of you. Call to action: "Book a 15-minute look at your office".
- **Meta, ~$10/day:** retarget demo-page visitors and people matched from our list. Needs the Meta pixel on the demo
  (ask the AI Office Flow code chat).
- **Google Search, ~$10/day:** exact phrases only, e.g. "kitchen business software", "joinery job management
  software", "cabinet maker software".

**Why January, not now:** shops are flat out in December and then shut. Money spent then is wasted.

**Ad copy (draft)**
> Headline: Ran a kitchen business for 25 years. Built the office I wanted.
> Text: No more "where are we up to?" calls. Website chat that books measures, numbered jobs, quotes signed online,
> payments into Xero, and customer and installer apps with your logo. Built in your business's name.
> Button: Book a 15-min look

**Video (you, 30-45 s, phone, in a workshop):** "I ran a kitchen business for over 25 years. The thing that wore me
down was the office: chasing quotes, and customers ringing to ask where their job was up to. So I built a system that
handles it, in your business's own name. If that sounds like your week, book 15 minutes and I'll show you."

**What you have to do:** about 1 hour (record the video, approve the ads, enter your card). The team drafts
everything, and you press go.
**Cost:** capped at $1,200 for the 4 weeks.
**How we measure it:** clicks, demo visits, booked looks, owner conversations and sales, by channel.
**Keep or drop:** stop any channel at $600 spent with no owner conversations. Keep a channel if it costs under about
$300 per owner conversation. Full review Fri 12 Feb.
**Watch out:** LinkedIn needs a minimum audience size, and many small-shop owners aren't active on LinkedIn. Meta
can't reliably target "business owners" any more. That's why this is a capped test, not a plan to scale up.
